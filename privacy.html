Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to The Narayanganj application ("the App"), developed as a public utility and local service directory for Narayanganj city.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App functions primarily as an informational directory (such as hospitals, clinics, doctor chambers, emergency contacts, and public transport). We do not require users to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (PII) to use the App.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App may store minimal preference data locally on your device (such as your saved bookmarks/favorites or cached directory data) to allow the App to load quickly and function offline. This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our external servers.

3. Third-Party Services and APIs

The App fetches public directory information through secure Google Sheets endpoints (OpenSheet API). These requests only retrieve directory content and do not transmit personal user data. If you use external navigation links (e.g., Google Maps) or make direct phone calls from the directory listings, those services are governed by their respective privacy terms.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The content provided is general city utility information suitable for all audiences.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published directly on this page with a revised date.
